A portfolio site designed just for you, that you update yourself.


This page edits the way your site does: click something and a panel opens right beside it.

Your site changes when you decide.

  1. It is made for you

    I design and code it from scratch, with its own animation and character. Nothing comes from a template.

  2. You edit it from home

    Open it with your access code, on a computer or a phone, with nothing to install. Click a text, an image, a colour.

  3. You publish it

    One button, and your visitors see the new version. You don’t have to ask anyone.

Foliome takes care of the rest.

Your photos
Send them as they are, even from your phone. They are straightened, made lighter, and their GPS location is removed.
All your files
PDFs, sounds, 3D models, archives: every format is accepted.
Your way back
Undo up to a hundred changes if you don’t like the result.
Your languages
One tab per language, beside each text to translate.
